## What changed during the walk

### The three fixes the phase was briefed with

1. **The team switch is finished.** `SprintRepository.teams(org, project)` reads
   `GET {org}/_apis/projects/{project}/teams?api-version=7.1` through the same day-long cache as
   the iterations, and `SprintPage` hands the list to the picker and re-resolves everything —
   iterations, columns, snapshot, burndown — when another team is picked, dropping the sprint
   from the route because it names an iteration the new team does not have. `SprintTeamRef`
   moved to `lib/data/models/sprint.dart` (the picker re-exports it) so the repository does not
   import a widget. Spike **s56** confirmed the route and that **all four puremedia projects
   have exactly one team**, so the switch row is correctly hidden everywhere here and the
   multi-team path stays test-only.

2. **The app-bar title leads with the sprint.** It was project-first and truncated to
   "DevOp… / Iteration 1 ·…". What the app bar actually leaves for the title on an iPhone is
   **84 dp** — back arrow, person filter and a three-segment pill take the rest — so the fix is
   four things together: the sprint name on the first line at the smaller title size on compact,
   the second line dropping the project there (two letters of a project the shell has already
   named are worth less than the dates), the caret at 20 dp with no side padding, and a 44 dp
   leading slot instead of 56. "Iteration 1 / 8–21 Sep", "Iteration 2 / 22 Sep – 5 Oct" and
   "Iteration 106 / Ended 8 days ago" now all fit whole. From medium up nothing was taken away:
   the project comes back on the second line at the larger size.

3. **The sparkline's weekend band** is half the alpha of the full chart's (0.03 against 0.06).
   At 48 dp over a four-day series the old value read as a grey slab; it now reads as a tint.

### S13, mid-walk (Kelly)
The sprint header is **gone from the Taskboard tab** at every width, and on the tablet the
burndown/capacity supporting pane beside the grid is gone with it, so the grid has the full
width and height. The header stays on Backlog and Burndown, the capacity strip on Backlog, and
the chart and its facts on Burndown — nothing was lost, it moved. `71-s13-landscape` and
`84-ipad-sprint` are the before-and-after worth looking at.

### Four defects the devices found

1. **"No burndown data" over four days of burndown.** One task with Remaining Work on it flips
   the header's rollup to hours, and the page was dropping the Analytics series whenever the
   unit was hours — because the verdict would otherwise have read "0.9 **h**/day behind" over a
   series that counts items. The two units are now separate (`SprintHeaderData.seriesUnit`): the
   Remaining tile stays in hours, the sparkline and the verdict stay in items.
2. **"Remaining 0 h" for a sprint with fourteen open tasks.** Clearing Remaining Work writes a
   real 0, so the rollup kept answering "hours" for ever after. Zero hours is the same answer as
   no hours: the header counts items again.
3. **A column header reading " remaining"** with no number, from the same cleared 0 — both
   column-subtitle helpers tested "did any card carry the field" instead of "is there anything
   to say". Fixed in `sprint_page.dart` and `taskboard_grid.dart`.
4. **Two sprint switches in a row / the app-bar title** were P-C's; what this phase added on top
   is that a **team** switch must also clear the route's iteration, or the page asks the new team
   for a sprint it does not have.

## Spike s56 (read-only, new)
`GET {org}/_apis/projects/{project}/teams?api-version=7.1` answers 200 with
`{count, value:[{id, name, url, description, identityUrl, projectName, projectId}]}` and takes
the project **name** as well as its id; no rate-limit cost header, 0.07–0.15 s per call. All
four puremedia projects have one team each. Recorded in `research/spikes/results/README.md`.
